# Juno: setup (2 minutes)

1. Download **Juno-Setup-x.y.z.exe** from https://github.com/moserkfr/juno/releases/latest and run it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the app just isn't code-signed).
3. Juno opens by itself (there's also a desktop icon). Click **NEW HERE** and sign up with the email you were invited with.
4. Open the verification email, click the link, then press **I'M VERIFIED ✓** in Juno and pick your character.
5. Done! Juno starts with Windows, lives in the tray, and updates itself.
